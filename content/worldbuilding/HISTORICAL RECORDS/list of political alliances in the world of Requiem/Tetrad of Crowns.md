The Tetrad of Crowns is a [[list of political alliances in the world of Requiem|political alliance]] in the continent of Vrumdale, composed by the four strongest Kingdoms in Vrumdale: [[Mezonyodia]],  [[Mabamalia]], [[Chiswestia]], and [[Prexum]]. Formed during  [[The War of Ages]], the Tetrad of crowns will head on to become the strongest political alliance in the continent



Honorable member:
- [[Ancan]] (through alliance of all four members, despite not being a monarchy)
