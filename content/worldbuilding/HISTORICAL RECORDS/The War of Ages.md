THE WAR OF THE AGES
[[Vrumdale]] vs [[Rouvryia]]
vrumdale lived in general peace. vrumdale's balance stems from celestial magic and mages in themselves, so with the rise in magical-technological advancements, the rise in popularity and union within the country, they were thriving. Vrumdale and Vrumdelian Leaders, specifically, did not appreciate the [[Gods]], but kept to themselves. 
The [[Gods]] ruled in Rouvryia, and grew stronger through veneration and prayer. They began attempting to conquer Vrumdale through thee power of prayer, mind control, and crusades to the coasts of countries like [[Pontia]], [[Chiswestia]], [[Prexum]], [[Mezonyodia]] 

The hearts of the deceased Major Gods now lie as trophies in Iulia for safekeeping, to keep another war from breaking if they were to resurface as corporeal.
