the calendar is divided in 9 months, 5 days per week, 8 weeks per month. 
	week (5 days) 
	diacom (10 days) 

Months:
1. OASIS maia
2. INTUNE arelia
3. AVDEA vayja
4. VOTDITE nia
5. KRIONE kaneas
6. RALVA dreva
7. THESYN thevaris
8. ZAGHEIA niraura
9. NELASIX qratia
