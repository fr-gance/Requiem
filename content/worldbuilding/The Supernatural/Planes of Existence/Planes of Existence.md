There are nine planes in the world of Requiem, all existing in opposition to one another.
- the [[Material Plane]] is the plane in which all the world operates. The planet itself; The World of Requiem. 
- [[Edrath]] is the home of the Primordial Dragons of Balance, previously accessed through a golden door above Skyhaven, comprising a paradise of megafauna and megaflora and comfort for those invited. It has now been sealed with the seven living Primordial Dragons inside, never to come out, having rescinded their responsibilities as Keepers of Balance[^1].
- The [[Celestial Plane]] is the plane of the Gods, where the Major Gods were originally created and where most of the other divinities sprung from, in due time. 
- [[The Feywild]] is home of all fey creatures and their closely related races, such as mages and elves. 
- The [[Elemental Plane]] is home to the elementals, but also to all the sub-planes, elemental spaces, and the planar tie to all dimensions, subdimensions, and pocket dimensions. this is the only plane tied to the Material Plane fully, while being its separate entity. 
- the [[Underworld]] is home to Demons and Devils alike. It works like a coin: one for each side, and the Demonic side (commonly known as Hell or Sinners' Afterlife) works in nine circles, each one ruled by a prince. The Devilish side (commonly referred to as The Tenth Circle) is a simple lawless pit. 
- [[The Mists]] used to be a plane 
- the [[Void]]
- [[Ninth Plane]]

The [[Celestial Plane]] exists in opposition to the [[Underworld]], the [[Material Plane]] in opposition to the [[Void]],  the [[Elemental Plane]] in opposition to [[The Feywild]], and [[Edrath]] in opposition to [[The Mists]]. 

The only plane that doesn't oppose another is the [[Ninth Plane]], also called the Beyond, or the Plane Of Chaos.
