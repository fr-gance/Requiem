Edrath is the parallel plane of existence where all Dragons originally came from. The doors to it, and the remaining seven Primordial Dragons of Balance, sealed themselves to Edrath after [[The Fall of Skyhaven]]. 

It used to exist in opposition to [[The Mists]], before it merged with Talxua (specifically Zarya-Stan) after the events of The Fall
